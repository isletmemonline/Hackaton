# Workflows
1. ST creates immutable INS/REP/REM work order for one equipment of an authorized customer; sole technician auto-assigned.
2. Before initial SSM decision, ST may cancel. Initial SSM approves to permit technician work or rejects to CANCELLED with reason.
3. Technician completes with nonblank explanation and at least one photo; operational status becomes COMPLETED, pending ST completion review.
4. ST approves completion; or rejects to CANCELLED with reason.
5. SSM gives final approval, closing order (terminal name CLOSED provisional); or rejects to CANCELLED with reason.
6. All approvals and actions appear in chronological detail timeline, with actor, time, outcome, reason. Technician evidence immutable.
7. A completed order awaiting review remains active for one-order-per-equipment enforcement.
